# Churn: a starter-onboarding problem, not a top-of-funnel problem

## Thesis

Monthly churn rose from **11.4% to 14.2%** over the last six months. **71% of that increase** is starter-plan accounts cancelling in their first 30 days.

## Evidence

- Cohort `<30d` churns at **2.3x** the rate of `>90d`
- Free→starter conversion is **down 38% MoM**
- Pro plan churn flat at 4.1%; enterprise n too small
- Top cancel reason text: *"couldn't get it set up"*

## Ask

One sprint of onboarding work targeted at **starter day 0 to 14**.

- Owner: `@growth`
- Success metric: starter-day-30 retention from **56% to 68%** by end of Q3
- Dependencies: small data hookup from `@platform` for activation events

## Out of scope

- Pricing changes (separate workstream)
- Free-tier limits (treat as input, not output)
- Enterprise churn (sample too small)
